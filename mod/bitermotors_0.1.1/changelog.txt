Version: 0.1.1
  Major Features:
    - First Biter Motors public-alpha release candidate.
    - Adds a complete Nauvis campaign from crash landing through physical AGI training.
    - Turns biter settlements into customers for EVs, Grid Batteries, and Bitertaxi service.
    - Adds terrestrial and orbital AI compute with physical power, cooling, and token logistics.
  Features:
    - Adds five drivable EV products, the battery-electric Cybertrain, and the remote-controlled eSpider.
    - The eSpider includes four Tesla guns, four Battery MK3 units, four exoskeletons, charger support, and limp-home reserve propulsion.
    - Clean battery routes use dedicated refining, extraction, and dry-electrode recipe artwork instead of module badges.
    - Keeps all four EV Charging Station tiers together in one crafting-picker row.
    - Adds Biterfactories, battery chemistry, recycling, high-density solar power, and Grid Batteries.
    - Adds bounded customer populations, charging commutes, Self-driving, and Summon EV.
    - Restores belt-capacity research, Battery MK3, and advanced asteroid crushing without removed planetary science.
    - Rehomes late Tesla weapon damage upgrades onto the Biter Motors AI Token economy.
  Info:
    - Requires Factorio and Space Age 2.1.20; updates hidden battery fuel items for the current prototype schema.
    - Source and packaged smoke tests pass on 2.1.20; full campaign and release qualification remain pending.
    - Reduces recurring charger and customer-market update spikes in large terrestrial saves.
    - This is alpha software. Balance, artwork, progression, and compatibility may change.
  Bugfixes:
    - Contested chargers share capacity in stall-sized rounds instead of starving equal neighboring settlements.
    - Settlement mood, growth, alerts, and inspectors use the same private-charging or Bitertaxi service-health rule.
    - Healthy taxi-only settlements grow; deficient neighbors do not inflate healthy charger expansion utilization.
    - Pending buyers are counted once across EV models when balancing Sales Offices between settlements.
    - Regenerates rewritten vanilla recycling outputs from terrestrial ingredients instead of removed planetary materials.
    - Damaged battery packs wait intact for a ten-pack batch and recover exactly 36 cells without productivity.
    - Shared Premium EVs and anonymous charger incidents no longer invent advanced battery chemistry.
    - Destroyed player vehicles yield one quality-matched body wreck plus known packs; mining does not duplicate salvage.
    - Construction bots no longer carry hidden vehicle drive-charge items into storage after deconstruction.
    - Physical purchases no longer exhaust untouched virtual prospects; replacement history stays distinct for each customer generation.
    - Virtual-only settlements and pending purchases survive population rebuilds and normal mod updates.
    - Sales Offices reserve buyers before starting a vehicle transaction without suppressing required EV paperwork.
